# Remaining hardware verification — 13 October 2026

This register was created before changing the assembly. It supersedes conclusions in the supplied audit only where explained here. No physical hardware was powered, inspected or measured. **The assembly is a conditional circuit reference, not a construction approval.**

VERIFIED means a directly examined document or genuinely performed check supports the stated fact, within its stated scope. CALCULATED means explicit assumptions and arithmetic; SIMULATED means an executed model with stated coverage; CONDITIONAL depends on stated hardware assumptions; UNVERIFIED lacks sufficient evidence. A verified IC fact does not verify a clone breakout or its connector positions.

## Physical verification sequence

1. Obtain exact identities, front/back labels and relevant schematics for every board, motor, battery, switch, fuse and connector. Trace terminals with a continuity meter while disconnected. Do not infer order from model geometry or wire colors.
2. Select protection and conductor ratings from actual load/fault evidence. Use a current-limited bench supply for initial work. F1 close to BAT+; F2 at the bus takeoff. Check polarity and insulation before applying power.
3. Isolate motor and ESP32. Set buck output with a DMM to 5.00V; verify under a known dummy load and scope ripple/dips. Confirm non-isolated ground and board supply rules. One supply source only.
4. Check resistor values and ADC voltages with a DMM before attaching GPIOs. Use calibrated ADC conversion, multi-point voltage/current calibration and characterized sampling/filtering. Keep all inputs unpowered when the ESP32 is unpowered.
5. Calibrate ACS at genuine zero primary current using an isolated primary path and independent regulated sensor/logic supply. Characterize gain, offset, noise and supply drift against a suitable reference. Reconnect the normal power topology only with supplies off.
6. Validate OLED ACK/address/full patterns and DS18B20 presence, conversion completion and CRC. Compare case temperature to a reference thermometer; keep missing/unvalidated values unavailable.
7. After motor/protection approval, capture startup and turnoff using a protected source, rated current probe/shunt and oscilloscope; evaluate suppression, reset behavior and temperature. Do not use a VRLA battery for an exploratory stall test.

An ERC or numerical model cannot close the physical-rating items. The final test report records the checks actually executed in this session.
