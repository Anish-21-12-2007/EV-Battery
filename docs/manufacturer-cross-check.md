# Manufacturer cross-check — 3 November 2026

The links below were opened and checked directly during this revision. This is documentary verification; no physical circuit has been tested.

| Manufacturer document | Verified scope | Consequence for this project |
|---|---|---|
| [Texas Instruments LM2596, Rev. G](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm2596.pdf), sections 7.1 and 7.3 | 4.5–40 V operating input; 45 V absolute maximum for the IC | Corrects the inherited “40 V absolute maximum” statement. The board's capacitor, inductor, diode and thermal limits still need their own identification. Absolute maximum is not an operating target. |
| [Texas Instruments INA219, Rev. G](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ina219.pdf), electrical tables and shunt register | 26 V operating bus, selectable ±40/80/160/320 mV shunt range, 10 µV shunt LSB, I²C interface | A 0.1 Ω shunt at 0.5 A produces 50 mV and does not fit ±40 mV gain. The 32 V register scale is not a 32 V operating rating. An INA219 requires a digital wiring revision. |
| [Texas Instruments INA226, Rev. C](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ina226.pdf), electrical table and shunt register | 36 V bus, approximately ±81.92 mV shunt range, 2.5 µV shunt LSB | These are different specifications from INA219. A 0.1 Ω shunt at 3 A gives 300 mV and overranges INA226. |
| [Allegro ACS712, Rev. 22](https://www.allegromicro.com/-/media/files/datasheets/acs712-datasheet.pdf), selection table and typical application | 30 A suffix has 66 mV/A nominal sensitivity, 5 V supply context, bypass and FILTER capacitors; not intended for automotive applications | Retain the documented scaler as a conditional educational example. It neither identifies a breakout nor establishes low-current measurement accuracy or suitability for an actual EV. |
| [Espressif classic ESP32 ADC guide, v4.2](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/v4.2/esp32/api-reference/peripherals/adc.html) | Suggested most-accurate range at 11 dB: 150–2450 mV; calibration required | The divider's calculated 2.348 V corner fits this specified context only. It does not prove surge or unpowered-GPIO safety. |
| [Espressif hardware schematic checklist](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32/schematic-checklist.html), ADC section | ADC1 preferred with Wi-Fi; 0.1 µF ADC filtering guidance; ±60 mV example after calibration at the cited range | Supports an error-budget example, not a claim of exact measurements. |
| [Espressif ESP32-DevKitC V4 guide](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32/esp32-devkitc/user_guide.html), power options and header table | Reference WROOM-32E board: J2.19 is 5 V, J2.1 is 3V3, J3.6 is GPIO21 and J3.3 is GPIO22. Only one USB/5 V-header/3V3-header source may power the board at a time. | Board-menu choice in Arduino IDE does not identify the installed carrier. Use this map only on the matching selected board; verify the actual board orientation and silk screen. |

The independent [electrical audit](advanced-circuit-audit.md) records the return-loop, unscheduled protection and interface issues. The separate [hardware proposal](hardware-build-proposal.md) selects concrete parts; its revised wiring must not be mixed with the inherited 60-wire assembly.
