## Part 2 — Summary of Open Hardware & Safety Gates (`TBD` — Do Not Guess)

No component rating below was fabricated. Each item must be resolved by inspecting the actual physical hardware and applying the selection equations in `05_Protection_and_Safety/` before construction:

1. **Battery (`BAT1`)**: Physical battery label, chemistry (SLA/AGM vs. $	ext{LiFePO}_4$ vs. Li-ion), maximum charging voltage, cutoff voltage, usable capacity at target discharge C-rate, and prospective short-circuit current ($I_{	ext{sc}}$).
2. **Fuses (`F1`, `FL`, `FM`, `F2`)**: Rated current ($I_{	ext{n}}$), DC voltage rating ($\ge 32	ext{ V DC}$), DC breaking capacity ($> I_{	ext{sc}}$), and time-current / $I^2t$ curve coordinated with downstream wire gauge and load inrush ([Littelfuse Fuse Fundamentals, `S6`](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/fuse-fundamentals-technical-paper?assetguid=82021200-8d3c-4449-843b-4959181a04af)).
3. **Master Switch (`SW1`)**: DC voltage and DC inductive/motor breaking current rating ([Omron Relay/Inrush FAQ, `S7`](https://www.ia.omron.com/support/faq/answer/36/faq02165/)).
4. **Conductors & Connectors (`WIRE`, `BUS+`, `JGND`, `J1`)**: Copper gauge (`AWG`/`mm²`), insulation temperature rating, route length (for $\le 2	ext{–}3\%$ round-trip voltage drop), and terminal ampacity.
5. **Light Branch (`L1`, `S_L`, `U_I_L`)**: Complete 12 V DC lamp wattage/inrush, matched current sensor `S_L` selection, and `GPIO32` signal conditioner `U_I_L`.
6. **Motor Branch (`M1`, `MDRV`, `S_M_BOARD`, `D1`)**: Motor rated/stall current, controller topology (unidirectional PWM vs. reversing H-bridge—which dictates whether a parallel diode `D1` is permissible or causes a short circuit!), and `S_M_BOARD` PCB trace/terminal rating.
7. **Electronics Branch (`U_BUCK_BOARD`, `U_ESP`, `U_OLED_BOARD`, `T1_PROBE`, `RP1`, `TVS1`)**: Buck converter board verification ($5.00	ext{ V}$ regulation and common `IN−`/`OUT−` ground continuity), `ESP32` carrier board pinout, `OLED` $3.3	ext{ V}$ $	ext{I}^2	ext{C}$ pullup verification, `DS18B20` lead identification, reverse-polarity protection (`RP1`), and bus transient clamp (`TVS1`).
