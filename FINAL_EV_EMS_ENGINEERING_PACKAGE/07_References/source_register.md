## Calculation conventions

- 1 US liquid gallon = 3.785411784 L.
- 1 international Btu = 0.00029307107 kWh.
- Resistor corner analysis assumes independent 1% extremes on top and bottom resistors.
- The 18 V value is a **design assumption**, not a value from S5 or a transient limit.
- No source supports the actual Light/Motor current, fuse rating, wire gauge, exact converter module, actual tariff or range estimate.
